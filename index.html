3. SCHOOL ACTIVITIES

A. Five Steps for Starting a Computer

  1. Connect the computer to power.
  2. Switch on the power supply.
  3. Press the computer power button.
  4. Wait for the computer to start.
  5. Enter your password if required.

B. Five Computer Laboratory Rules

  • Do not eat in the computer laboratory.
  • Do not drink near computers.
  • Keep the laboratory clean.
  • Handle computer equipment carefully.
  • Follow the teacher's instructions.

C. Computer Terms and Their Meanings

Hardware
The physical parts of a computer.
Software
Programs that make a computer work.
Internet
A worldwide network that connects computers.
